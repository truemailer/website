How it works

Send the email

2

FAQ

Common questions

What is a disposable email address?

A disposable email address is a temporary inbox created to receive a single message, often used to bypass signup verification or trial limits without providing a real address.

How does Truemailer detect disposable emails?

Truemailer checks the domain of an email address against a continuously updated list of known disposable and temporary email providers, then returns a real-time verdict through a simple API call.

Is Truemailer free to use?

Yes. The free tier is meant for testing and low-volume use, with paid plans available once you need higher throughput.

Does Truemailer store the emails I check?

Truemailer only uses submitted addresses to return a verdict and does not sell or share the data you send it.
