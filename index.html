How it works

Three steps to a cleaner signup form

1

Send the email

POST the address to /verify the moment your user submits your signup form.

2

Get a verdict

Truemailer checks the domain against its disposable-email list and returns a verdict and reason.

3

Allow or block

Use the response to accept the signup or ask the user for a permanent email address.
